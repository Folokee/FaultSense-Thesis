\chapter{Discussion}
\label{ch:discussion}

\section{Interpretation of Results}
\label{sec:interpretation}

Interpret the findings presented in Chapter~\ref{ch:results}. Explain what the
results mean in the context of the research questions. Discuss whether the
results support or contradict the initial hypotheses or expectations.

\section{Comparison with Prior Work}
\label{sec:comparison}

Compare your results with those reported in the literature (see
Chapter~\ref{ch:literature-review}). Highlight similarities, differences,
and possible reasons for discrepancies.

\section{Implications}
\label{sec:implications}

\subsection{Theoretical Implications}
\label{sec:theo-implications}

Discuss the theoretical contributions and implications of your findings.
How do they advance the understanding of the research area?

\subsection{Practical Implications}
\label{sec:prac-implications}

Discuss the practical applications and implications of your research.
How can practitioners benefit from your findings?

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

Acknowledge the limitations of your research, including methodological
constraints, data limitations, and scope boundaries. Be honest and
transparent about what the research does and does not address.

\section{Chapter Summary}
\label{sec:disc-summary}

Summarize the key discussion points.
