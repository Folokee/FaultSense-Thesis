\chapter{Literature Review}
\label{ch:literature-review}

\section{Overview}
\label{sec:lit-overview}

This chapter reviews the existing body of knowledge relevant to the research
problem. It provides a critical analysis of prior work, identifies gaps in
the literature, and positions the current research within the broader
academic context.

\section{Theoretical Framework}
\label{sec:theoretical-framework}

Present the theoretical foundations that underpin your research. Define
key concepts, models, and theories that are central to understanding
your work.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related-work}

\subsection{Theme 1}
\label{sec:lit-theme1}

Discuss the first major theme or category of related work. Synthesize
findings from multiple sources and highlight patterns, agreements, or
disagreements in the literature.

\subsection{Theme 2}
\label{sec:lit-theme2}

Discuss the second major theme. Compare and contrast different
approaches, methodologies, or findings.

\subsection{Theme 3}
\label{sec:lit-theme3}

Discuss the third major theme. Identify limitations in existing
approaches and areas that warrant further investigation.

\section{Research Gap}
\label{sec:research-gap}

Synthesize the gaps identified in the literature and clearly articulate
the specific gap that your thesis addresses. Explain why addressing this
gap is important and how your work contributes to filling it.

\section{Chapter Summary}
\label{sec:lit-summary}

Briefly summarize the key takeaways from the literature review and how
they inform the rest of the thesis.
